## 1. Project Nexus: The Integrated Innovation Funnel (SC Ventures)

**Case:** *SC Ventures* (INSEAD case study, Anderson, 2024). **Format:** 7-slide deck pitched to an executive board.

**The challenge:** SC Ventures is Standard Chartered's unit for venture building, corporate venture capital and innovation labs. It had incubated more than 30 ventures, but one problem remained: how to turn a global workforce of 80,000+ employees into a visible source of innovation, rather than relying on a small group of intrapreneurs.

**My proposal:** Project Nexus, a decentralised, AI-driven innovation funnel that turns raw employee insights into ventures co-created with external fintech partners. It builds on the existing Incubation, Acceleration and Commercialisation pipeline and upgrades the Fintech Bridge from a matching portal into a co-development sandbox.

**Strategy and frameworks**
- **Organisational readiness:** Uses the bank's regulatory trust as a "safe harbor" so employees can experiment without disrupting core banking operations.
- **Timing strategy:** A fast-follower approach that reduces pioneer risk by adapting proven external technologies once the market is ready.
- **Ecosystem strategy:** Strategic alliances pairing internal "Problem Owners" with external "Solution Builders".
- **Balanced Scorecard:** Targets across four perspectives, including 15% venture-led revenue growth within 3 years, 70% customer satisfaction for new digital tools over 5 years, 70,000+ employees registered and active, and successful AI model deployment within the 18-month funnel.
- **Protecting value:** An "open-core" appropriability model. The platform stays open to maximise participation, while utility patents protect ventures that survive the final bootcamp.
- **Responsible AI:** ISO/IEC 42001 for explainable, bias-aware team matching, and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework to secure sensitive employee data.

**Implementation: a four-level funnel over 18 months**

| Level | Timeline | Key activities |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | Months 0-3 | Micro-pitch challenges: one-minute pain-point submissions from staff |
| 2. Synthesis | Months 4-6 | AI squad formation: clustering ideas and matching employees with external fintech mentors |
| 3. Validation | Months 7-9 | Virtual bootcamp for the top 5% of squads, with real client feedback |
| 4. Scaling | Months 10-18 | Commercialisation: seed funding and global rollout for successful ventures |

**Professional communication:** The deck closes with a reflection on peer review. Feedback made me shift the pitch from the technical mechanics of AI to the human side: lowering the barrier to entry for non-technical staff and building psychological safety, so the idea is culturally scalable as well as technically sound.

**Skills and tools:** Innovation strategy, ecosystem and partnership design, Balanced Scorecard, responsible AI governance, roadmap planning, executive pitching (PowerPoint).

## 2. PeopleCert: Platform-Driven Corporate Upskilling Strategy

**Case:** *PeopleCert: A Greek Unicorn Leveraging Digital for Organizational Efficiency and Upskilling* (INSEAD case study, Evgeniou et al., 2025). **Format:** Case analysis report (1,954 words) with three analytical sections and a conclusion.

**The challenge:** PeopleCert is a professional certification and language assessment company. It faces intense competition in language assessment from established giants (IELTS, ETS, Cambridge Assessment, Pearson), new AI-enabled digital entrants, and a growing shift toward micro-credentials and employer-led training that bypass formal exams.

**My recommendation:** Move from a content-owning gatekeeper to an open, platform-driven corporate upskilling ecosystem, using a strategy of **Focused Differentiation**.

**Report structure**
1. **Industry dynamics:** Porter's Five Forces extended with complementors. The 2021 Axelos acquisition removed the firm's dependence on third-party licences (about 90% of revenue), but IP control alone is not enough to stay defensible.
2. **Strategy and competitive advantage:**
   - Technology licensing to access advanced AI, and non-equity alliances with HCM and LMS platforms instead of joint ventures.
   - Core investments in generative AI adaptive assessment, predictive analytics for B2B buyers, and secure APIs for the partner ecosystem.
   - Balanced Scorecard targets: 90% annual B2B customer retention within 24 months, at least 35% of revenue from B2B platform integration contracts within 24 months, 99% grading accuracy, partner API onboarding cut from months to under 14 business days, and 100% compliance within 12 months.
3. **Implementation plan:**
   - A Cross-Functional Platform Innovation Unit reporting to the Executive Board, run with Project Charters and Contract Books.
   - Three phases: set up the unit and API scoping (0-12 months), build the AI engines and sign alliances (12-24 months), and reach full platform scale (24-36 months).
   - A Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) covering two risks: algorithmic grading bias (mitigated with an independent responsible AI ethics board) and IP leakage or exam fraud (mitigated with zero-trust security and third-party ISO/IEC audits).

**Skills and tools:** Porter's Five Forces, generic competitive strategy, technology collaboration modes, Balanced Scorecard, organisational design, phased roadmaps, FMEA risk analysis, academic referencing.
